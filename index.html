From Pelli Choopulu to Saptapadi

✦ ♥ ✦

Every great story begins quietly —
a Pelli Choopulu, a few shy smiles,
and two families praying with full hearts.

A first hello became a conversation,
a conversation became laughter,
and laughter grew into a friendship
that felt like coming home.

In small moments of understanding —
a glance that said enough,
a silence that felt like comfort —
two hearts found something rare and true.

Now, with the blessings of our elders
and the love of our families,
we walk the seven sacred steps of Saptapadi —
seven vows, seven prayers,
one lifetime together.

Hand in hand, we will honour every tradition,
cherish every moment,
and write our forever — together.
